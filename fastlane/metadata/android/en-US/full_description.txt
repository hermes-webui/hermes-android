Hermes WebUI for Android brings your self-hosted Hermes WebUI workspace to Android without turning it into a second product. Hermes WebUI stays the real application; this app adds the Android capabilities that matter on a phone or tablet.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Hardened WebView that only loads the Hermes servers you configure</li>
<li>Multiple server profiles with quick switching</li>
<li>Share text, links, and files from other apps into Hermes</li>
<li>Uploads, downloads, and voice input</li>
<li>Native notifications for Hermes activity and approvals</li>
<li>Encrypted local settings and client-certificate (PKCS#12) support</li>
<li>Sign-in through OAuth/OIDC and reverse-proxy login pages</li>
</ul>

<b>Requirements</b>
This app requires a running Hermes WebUI server (https://github.com/nesquena/hermes-webui). On first launch, enter the URL of your server.

<b>Privacy</b>
The app talks only to the Hermes servers you configure. This F-Droid build contains no Google libraries, analytics, or tracking, and it installs side by side with the Google Play and GitHub builds.
