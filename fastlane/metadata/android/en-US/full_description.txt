Bring the official Hermes Agent WebApp or Hermes WebUI to Android with a native-feeling companion. Your server's web frontend stays the real application; this app adds the Android capabilities that matter on a phone or tablet. The official WebApp does not require a separate Hermes WebUI installation.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Hardened WebView that only loads the Hermes servers you configure</li>
<li>Multiple server profiles with quick switching and automatic blue Agent / gold WebUI badges</li>
<li>Android Back opens native Settings from the official WebApp without reloading your workspace</li>
<li>Choose official Hermes Agent or Hermes WebUI launcher artwork independently of your servers</li>
<li>Share text, links, and files from other apps into Hermes</li>
<li>Uploads, downloads, and voice input</li>
<li>Android-backed browser notifications; background session activity and approval actions for Hermes WebUI</li>
<li>Encrypted local settings and client-certificate (PKCS#12) support</li>
<li>Sign-in through OAuth/OIDC and reverse-proxy login pages</li>
</ul>

<b>Requirements</b>
This app requires either the official Hermes Agent browser-hosted Desktop workspace (v0.22.6 or newer, with Web Access enabled) or a Hermes WebUI server (https://github.com/nesquena/hermes-webui). Enter its URL and sign in normally. Use HTTPS for microphone access. Android does not enable Web Access on the server.

The official WebApp owns its chat, tabs and voice UI. Official gateway-WebSocket background monitoring, WebUI-style session deep links, blob/data-URL exports and notification clicks requiring a page-local JavaScript callback are not supported natively.

<b>Privacy</b>
The app talks only to the Hermes servers you configure. This F-Droid build contains no Google libraries, analytics, or tracking, and it installs side by side with the Google Play and GitHub builds.
